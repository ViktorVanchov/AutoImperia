A FEW USEFUL ANSWERS

Before you get behind the wheel.

Can I book a test drive?

Select “Test drive” in the contact form and mention the car and preferred time. This project prepares a downloadable enquiry only; it does not make a booking.

How do I sell or trade in a car?

Use “List your car” to add a local listing, then manage its details in My garage. For this demo, listings stay in your browser and are not shared with a dealer.

What should I check on a used car?

Ask for service records, review the condition and mileage, and consider an independent inspection. The sample listings here are not verified vehicles for sale.

Are the prices and vehicles real offers?

No. Vehicles, prices, staff, and business details illustrate a fictional dealership for a student assignment. No payment or purchase is available.

THE AUTOIMPERIA JOURNAL

Privacy & storage

This student project has no analytics, advertising, account system, or server-side contact database.

Your garage and saved cars use local storage in this browser. Contact form details stay on the page until you download an enquiry or leave the page; they are not sent to us.

Cookie preferences are remembered locally. If you allow preferences, your selected language is remembered too. Rejecting optional preferences removes the saved language choice.

Car images load from Unsplash and fonts from Google Fonts. Those providers receive connection information, including your IP address, when their resources load. GitHub Pages hosts the website.

You can clear website data in your browser settings to remove saved cars and preferences.
